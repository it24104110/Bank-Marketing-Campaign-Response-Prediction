  - The default 0.5 is not optimal: Max F1 raises F1 for every model

  - Each rule is a business trade-off (RF):
      Max F1:       call 14.6% -> find 60.7% of subscribers, conversion 46.7%
      Capacity 20%: call 20.1% -> find 67.1%, conversion 37.7%
      Recall >=70%: call 28.0% -> find 73.2%, conversion 29.4%
      vs calling everyone: conversion 11.3%

6. BUSINESS TRANSLATION                                             [fig16]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Calling everyone:            8,236 calls -> 928 subscribers (conversion 11.3%)
  Random Forest, Max F1:       1,206 calls (14.6%) -> 563 subscribers (60.7%)
                               conversion 46.7% = 4.1x calling everyone
  Random Forest, Capacity 20%: 1,653 calls (20.1%) -> 623 subscribers (67.1%)
                               conversion 37.7% = 3.3x calling everyone
  XGBoost, Max F1:             1,152 calls (14.0%) -> 548 subscribers (59.1%)
                               conversion 47.6% = 4.2x
  XGBoost, Capacity 20%:       1,632 calls (19.8%) -> 616 subscribers (66.4%)
                               conversion 37.7% = 3.3x
  -> With RF at 20% capacity the bank makes 80% fewer calls, still reaches
     two thirds of subscribers, and wasted calls fall from 7,308 to 1,030.
  -> Trade-off: about one third of subscribers (305) would not be called.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  year   test rows  yes-rate   ROC-AUC: LR    DT     RF     XGB     PR-AUC lift: LR   DT    RF    XGB
  2008     5,566      4.49%            0.560  0.537  0.588  0.589                1.29  1.50  2.21  2.13

  FINDINGS
  - Within every year, ranking is weaker than the overall ROC-AUC (0.81):
    part of the model's skill is telling GOOD PERIODS from BAD PERIODS
    (economic conditions), not only good clients from bad clients.
  - 2008 (first campaign period, almost no previous-contact history):
    weak within-year ranking (~0.59). 2009, when contact history exists:
    strong (0.79). 2010: good (0.74) but only 393 rows.
  - RF is best or tied-best in every year; its advantage over LR is
    largest in 2008 (PR-AUC lift 2.21 vs 1.29).
  - Confirms the Step 4 limitation: the model works best when trained on
    recent data that includes contact history; it must be retrained as
    conditions change, and within a single campaign the expected ranking
    quality is lower than the headline 0.81.
